# Day 9 — ZAMIN FK 2–2 STAR BOYS: match film + "yangi menejer" call

Format: 9:16, ~85 s. Story scenes in the club's cartoon style (Rustam aka, echki, chips, bubbles) over plates of the upgraded 3D stadium; the four goals are 3D clips from the same stadium.

## Assets used (checked in the repo)
- Stadium: `web/stadium/template.html` → `web/stadium/dist/index.html`, the version after the commit "Stadium: add upper seating tier and raise the roof" (11 Nov) — the bigger, taller one. Goals and all background plates are rendered from it.
- Player model: `web/stadium/models/player/player.glb` (+ `player_walk.glb`), names and numbers printed with the page's own shirt printer.
- ZAMIN FK lineup: club poster of 13 Nov, saved as `assets/zamin_lineup_2026-11-13.jpg`.
- STAR BOYS lineup: the owner's list (Varta 1 GK, Mahmud 10, Kamron — no number, Usmon 2, Ulug‘bek 4, Amirhon 3, Alpomosh 5, Sherzod 3, Bekzod 10, Azamat 12, Mirjalol 13). Kamron's shirt has no number. STAR BOYS kit colour (red) is a placeholder: no kit reference was in the repo.
- Crest, sprites, fonts, crowd bed, whistle, music: same files as Days 3–8.
