GENERAL FORMAT (all videos)
======================================================================
- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, MP4. Language: Uzbek (Latin).
- Structure (Playbook): HOOK -> PAYOFF/RESULT -> MATCH/TURN -> CONSEQUENCE -> QUESTION (+ outro hook).
- Daily cycle: Ask -> Close -> Show -> Carry. Viewers vote in comments; the next video shows the result.
- Mechanics (always labelled "VIRTUAL"): 1 follower = 1 virtual fan; 1 like = 15 000 so'm
  in the club fund. Start fund: 15 000 000 so'm.
- Voice: narrator "SARHAD-SIZ" (ElevenLabs voice rwtcHtZgYU6HN2zLNBlf), mono narration only.
  Day 3 used Poyo ElevenLabs V4 for some lines.
- Visual style: realistic 3D-style character sprites (cut-out, transparent background) over
  stadium/office backgrounds; slow zoom/pan (Ken Burns), parallax, blur behind cards.
- Animation engine: tools/anim (HTML frames -> ffmpeg). Effects: stamp, pop, slideL/R, drop, peek,
  idle bob/hop/sway/shake/chew/talk; number counters that roll up.
- Typography/brand: navy #0D2557, blue #1F4FB8, yellow #FFD23F, white; heavy bold titles;
  yellow "chips" (pill labels); word-by-word captions with highlighted key words (yellow).
- Safe zones: text kept in top/center; right ~150 px and bottom ~350 px left free for app buttons.
- Recurring characters/gags: Rustam aka (strict coach, whistle), Bekzod (striker, celebrates before
  scoring), Jasur (goalkeeper, lost glove/excuses), Aziz (midfielder, serious face), the stadium
  goat (white-grey, broken horn, blue-white scarf; unnamed), Doston and Sherzod (defenders).
  President narrates only (no approved image).
- Recurring end element: "+ OBUNA BO'LING" (follow) button pulsing.
- Pre-post checklist: first frame understandable without sound; one question, one-word answer;
  deadline stated; one rewatchable detail; "virtual" stated; captions outside button zones.
