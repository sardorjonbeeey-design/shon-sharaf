# Open Questions

Everything here is OPEN unless explicitly approved. Do not implement a proposed value as a final rule without owner approval.

## Rules and balance
- Starting club budget in UZS.
- Initial squad size and reserve count.
- Player pool size and generation method.
- Quality category names and rating distribution.
- Player attributes and overall-rating formula.
- Potential scale, upgrade limits and upgrade cost curve.
- Development-point rewards from matches and missions.
- Mission cadence, difficulty and anti-abuse limits.
- Transfer-market availability, refresh and pricing.
- Player wages, contracts and recurring costs.
- Club progression beyond player development.

## Competition and simulation
- League format and maximum number of clubs.
- Single or double round-robin.
- Season length, fixture cadence and tie-break rules.
- Handling clubs joining mid-season.
- Match duration and speed controls.
- Match-event model and probability formula.
- Influence of formation, tactics, positions, fatigue and fitness.
- Match simulation validation and replay.

## Product and technology
- Long-term interface: standalone website, native app, Telegram Mini App or multiple interfaces.
- Authentication, backend, database, hosting and deployment for an independent game service.
- Crest file limits and storage method.
- Whether local save is sufficient for the first prototype.
- Future integration boundaries, if ever requested.

Build isolated prototypes and use tests to answer questions where possible. Keep suggestions labelled PROPOSED until approved.
